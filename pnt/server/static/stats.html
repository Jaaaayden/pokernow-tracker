The postflop columns follow the street you pick, and all four turn on the previous street's aggressor: the player either is them (C-Bet) or is acting against them (Fold, Raise, Lead) — hover a column header for what each one counts. Fold vs C-Bet and Raise C-Bet share one denominator, the c-bets that player faced, so with calls they add to 100%. Note that a lead removes the street from the c-bet columns entirely: the aggressor never got to c-bet, so they take no missed chance, and folding or raising against a lead is counted nowhere. A rate is blank when the player never reached that spot; hover any rate to see the sample behind it. Click a player for their range chart in the same spot.
