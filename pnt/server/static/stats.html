Postflop columns follow the street you pick. Hover a column header or open the key below the table for what each one counts.

  • C-Bet, Fold, Raise and Lead are measured against the previous street's aggressor. Agg % is not: it counts every action on the street.
  • Fold vs C-Bet + Raise C-Bet + calls = 100% of the c-bets faced.
  • A lead takes the street out of the c-bet columns: no c-bet chance for the aggressor, and no fold or raise for anyone else.
  • A blank rate means they never reached the spot. Hover a rate for its sample size.
  • Folds by size adds how often each player folds to each size of bet, highlighted where they fold more than the pool's MDF allows. The fold equity table under this one is the whole pool.

Fold equity the whole pool

How often each size of bet got everyone to fold, against the fold rate a pure bluff that size needs (break-even), and how often players fold facing each size, against the minimum defense frequency (MDF). Every player, whatever the min-hands setting; the spot applies. Highlighted: a size that showed a profit as a pure bluff, or a pool that folds more than the bet demands, on 10+ hands. No cards are needed, so every bet counts; bluffs that got folds are never shown, so this says how often a size won, not what it was.
